# CIA Triad

The CIA Triad is a foundational model in information security, illustrating three core principles that help protect data:

```
          (DATA / Information)
               /       \
              /         \
Confidentiality           Availability
              \         /
               \       /
                \     /
                Integrity
```

---

## Confidentiality
- **Definition:** Ensures that only authorized parties can access information.  
- **Key Points:**
  - Permits authorized access to information.
  - Protects information from unauthorized disclosure.
  - Related terms and concepts:
    - **PII** (Personally Identifiable Information)
    - **PHI** (Protected Health Information)
    - **Sensitivity** (sensitive data classification)

---

## Integrity
- **Definition:** Ensures that data remains accurate, complete, and unmodified unless authorized.  
- **Key Points:**
  - Detects alterations that have occurred.
  - Ensures that information is correct in storage, transit, and processing.
  - Maintains authenticity, completeness, and accuracy.
  - Protects against both malicious and accidental modifications.

---

## Availability
- **Definition:** Ensures systems and data are accessible when needed.  
- **Key Points:**
  - Provides timely and reliable access to information.
  - Data should be accessible and usable by authorized users where and when it is needed.
  - Involves system capacity, redundancy, backups, and fault tolerance.
  - Aims to minimize downtime and latency for critical resources.
